# Home

Start here. Everything in this vault is reachable from this note.

## What goes where

- **Daily** – one note per day you open Obsidian. Auto-created. Dump everything here first, sort later.
- **School** – class notes and Arduino lab notes, plus one hub note per subject (Robotics, Science, Social Studies, Math).
- **Projects** – anything with a deadline or a finish line: Sumo robot, Robotic arm, Bloque Cero.
- **Topics** – one note per thing that keeps coming up: a part (L298N), an idea (PWM), a planet (Jupiter). When you click a link to a note that doesn't exist yet, it is created here.
- **Templates** – daily, class, lab, project. Insert them, don't edit them.

## Three rules

1. Capture goes in today's daily note. Sorting happens once a week, not in the moment.
2. Every note links to at least one other note.
3. If the same word shows up in three notes, it becomes a Topic note.
